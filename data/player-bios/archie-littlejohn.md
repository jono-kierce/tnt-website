The fiery player is well known for his ability to affect opposition player's mentally, and famously was once told by the commissioner he needed to tone it down for this reason.

Littlejohn earnt MVP honours in TNT's inaugural year, (along with [Sharrock](luke-sharrock.qmd) and [Kierce](jonathan-kierce.qmd)) after a powerful hitting display, leading the league in winners for the season with 39. The pair would finish 5th (4-4)

The second iteration of TNT looked far more promising- drafting tall player [Will Mumme](will-mumme.qmd), the pairing of Mumme at the net while Littlejohn rallied proved lethal, the pair finishing 2nd (6-2). Dreams of a championship were shattered though when 7th-placed Pink pounced in the quarters to knock them out.

Littlejohn missed the 3rd season with an ACL injury. The fourth season he redrafted Mumme but the pair struggled to find success, finishing last with only one win.
