Simpson is TNT's biggest 'What could have been'.
His first seaon with [Angus Hume](angus-hume.qmd) saw great success. Simpson only managed to make 4 from the 8 games, but earnt best-on honours in all 4 and the pair finished 2nd (6-2). Simpson would make the final and had championship points, but the two couldn't get it done, finishing runners up. Simpson earnt Finals MVP with 11 votes.

The second season was more difficult for Simpson. Drafting the Greeks, Simpson found it hard to find consistency without a regular partner, and ended up 8th (2-6).

Simpson missed both season 3 (injury) and 4 (wedding). Controversially, he was allowed to play in the Season 4 Semi Finals in place of [Luke Sharrock](luke-sharrock.qmd), and got Pink into the Finals where they would ultimately win the championship.
