Ethan Seamer's career opened with a bang: an 87.5% win rate in Season 1, the best of any regular that year, built on aggressive baseline hitting and a knack for winning tight sets. Four seasons and 32 matches later, he sits among the league's most prolific shot-makers — 142 career winners, third all-time — and its most watchable players. The catch: nobody in league history has committed more unforced errors (296), and the count has risen every single season, from under seven a game to nearly thirteen. His Season 3 stat lines were extraordinary in both directions, including a league-high 5.3 winners a game alongside double-digit errors. Eight best-on-court awards say the ceiling is still elite; the win rate's slide to 29% in Season 4 says the floor has dropped too. If Seamer ever tames the error count, look out.
