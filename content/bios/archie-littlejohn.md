Joint inaugural MVP, media mogul, and author of the most dramatic retirement in league history — announced at approximately 6:13am with a statement requesting privacy "during these dark hours." Archie Littlejohn's alleged ACL rupture cost him all of Season 3 on the court, but gave TNT its golden age of analysis: Chon's Champs and Archie's Abysmals became the league's most feared weekly institutions, and his slide-heavy highlight reel kept the memories alive. The playing résumé is serious — 12 votes in that shared Season 1 MVP, a 10-winner fill-in cameo that remains one of the great guest shifts, and a Season 2 finals run with Will Mumme. His Season 4 comeback as Yellow captain saw him make Mumme the number one pick "2 years running," a loyalty the ladder did not reward — but Yellow's one win produced more content than most teams' six. Power, party-goer, punctual, prolific: the pocket rocket endures.
