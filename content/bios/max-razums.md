The best win rate in TNT history — 86%, twelve wins from fourteen games — belongs not to an MVP but to Max "the Punj" Razums, the towering Latvian whose scouting report lists his key weapons as staggering height, net play, comedic value and "lots of body hair." Twice drafted by Luke Sharrock, twice a minor premier's partner, twice a semi-finalist, never a grand finalist: the Punj's Yellow and Red campaigns both ended one match short of the big stage, with reports that his captain "lost faith in the power hitter" hotly disputed by the Razums camp. Young, hungry, ambitious, and statistically the most reliable winner the league has ever seen. TNT awaits the comeback — and so does the record book, which he technically already tops.
