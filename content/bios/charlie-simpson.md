Charlie Simpson doesn't play often — just 11 matches across three seasons — but when he does, the highlight reel fills up fast. His Season 2 campaign produced a scarcely believable 6.8 winners per game, the highest single-season rate in league history, and his career winner-to-error ratio of 0.97 sits at the very top of the all-time list: almost uniquely among Tuesday night regulars, Simpson hits roughly as many winners as he makes mistakes. The scoreboard hasn't always kept up — that monster Season 2 came with a 33% team win rate — but individual recognition certainly has: eight best-on-court awards from just 11 outings is the best strike rate in the competition, and his 3.3 votes per game leads the league outright. A 64% career win rate rounds out the résumé. The league's part-timer with a full-timer's trophy cabinet.
