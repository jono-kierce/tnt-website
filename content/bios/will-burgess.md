No player's wins are celebrated quite like Will Burgess's — his first of Season 4 earned a triple-siren "🚨 WILL BURGESS W 🚨" announcement from the league's official channels. The story explains the noise: one of the inaugural season's opening-night players, Burgess was sidelined indefinitely with patellar tendonitis after a single round, beginning an injury saga (shared with his other employer, the Carlton Football Club) that swallowed the better part of two seasons. The scouting reports never wavered, praising a serve so violent it can't be fully quoted on a family website, and champion data boldly projected a finals charge. Season 4 delivered the comeback: drafted by the Commissioner himself, Burgess helped power Red up to second on the ladder mid-season, forcing errors in bunches — eight in one match against Pink — before a narrow quarter-final loss to Navy. The body finally held. The league is on notice.
