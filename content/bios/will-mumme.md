Back-to-back number one draft pick — the only player the captains simply refuse to let slide. Years of what the scouting report tactfully called "dirty bulk" produced one of TNT's most powerful hitters: fourth on the all-time winners list, with a highlight tape full of brute-force put-aways and some of the league's best mic'd-up content. Mumme's career began in the deep end as the inaugural Green captain, where a "please explain" over alleged scoring inaccuracies set the tone for a franchise that struggled on the scoreboard but never in the headlines. Since then he's been everywhere: a Season 2 finals run with Archie Littlejohn, a Season 3 campaign with the Commissioner, and a Season 4 reunion with Archie that even saw him produce a tournament highlight — taking out Shayl Inlander — in a finals series he wasn't playing in. The semi-final breakthrough is coming. The power never left.
