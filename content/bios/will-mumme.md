Few players have transformed their game like Will Mumme. He arrived in Season 1 as a cautious counter-puncher averaging barely 1.4 winners a game; by Season 4 he was the most prolific attacking player in the entire league at 5.9 a game — a fourfold increase that reflects a complete rebuild of his tennis. Season 2 was the breakthrough: 67% wins, the second-best efficiency among regulars, and a top-five MVP season. The full package hasn't quite assembled since — his Season 4 firepower coincided with a bruising 1-8 team record, making him the league's unluckiest player — but 127 career winners across 29 games tell the story of a genuine shot-maker. Six best-on-court awards and stints with five different teams later, Mumme enters Season 5 as the classic breakout candidate: the weapons are all there, and the results surely have to follow.
