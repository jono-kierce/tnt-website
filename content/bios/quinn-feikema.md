Commissioner. Founder. Videographer. Trophy procurement officer. Occasionally, tennis player. Quinn Feikema is the man without whom none of this exists — the drafts, the finals trailers, the stats machine, the Feikema family's legendary hosting of Presentation Night all run through him. The corruption, fraud and laundering rumours that swirl around his administration are, as the TNT Times notes, "not defamatory, it's well documented," and his stringent denial of the #justiceforpink fill-in scandal remains a masterclass in crisis management. On court, the Commissioner has logged more games than almost anyone, and after early seasons spent propping up the ladder, Season 4 brought the revival: leading the league in errors forced, dragging Red to as high as second before a quarter-final exit. His second-place ranking in career double faults is best understood as a workload statistic. Every league needs a heartbeat. TNT's wears the whistle and the racquet at once.
