Quinn Feikema's career is the league's great redemption arc. Season 1 was brutal: nine matches, nine losses, and barely a winner and a half per game. Most players would have quietly disappeared; Feikema instead became one of only a handful to appear in all four seasons, grinding out improvement year on year. The winners per game have climbed every season (1.6 → 3.0 → 2.9 → 3.25), the serve has become a genuine weapon — his six aces led all comers in Season 4 — and the results have followed, with a career-best 62.5% win rate and a top-three MVP finish this season. At 36 career matches he trails only Jonathan Kierce for appearances, a testament to durability across six different team colours. Once the league's easy-beat, Feikema is now the player nobody wants to see across the net on a Tuesday night.
