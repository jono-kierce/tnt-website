If Tuesday Night Tennis has a face, it's Jonathan Kierce. The league's all-time leader in appearances (37), winners (191) and best-on-court awards (18), Kierce has been a fixture across all four seasons and the benchmark everyone else measures themselves against. His Season 3 campaign remains the finest individual season the competition has seen: an 82% win rate, 5.4 winners a game, more winners than errors, and just a single double fault all year. Remarkably disciplined on serve for such an aggressive player, he pairs relentless shot-making with genuine consistency — a combination nobody else has managed across four seasons. Season 4 told a different story: his personal numbers stayed elite while the wins dried up, proof that even the league's best can't carry a court alone. Whatever colour shirt he's wearing — and he's worn four — Kierce remains the man to beat.
