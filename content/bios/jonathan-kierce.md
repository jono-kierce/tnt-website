The most decorated player in TNT history, and it isn't especially close. Two championships (Season 1 with Ethan Seamer, Season 3 as one half of "Lob City" with Damon Maurice), two MVPs including the Season 3 clean sweep — MVP, Finals MVP with 11 of a possible 12 votes, and the minor premiership — plus a joint share of the inaugural award. The tall left-hander leads the all-time winners count by a distance, has taken the season Winners title three years running, and produced a TNT first: a full match with zero errors and zero double faults. Season 4 may have been his finest work anyway — after drafting "loose cannon" Lachy Godden amid unconfirmed bribery rumours, he dragged White from seventh on the ladder to the grand final, falling only to Pink's perfect season in a final-set tiebreak. Champion, statistician, website author, and the man Jimmy Gorton cannot escape in finals.
