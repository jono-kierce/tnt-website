Nobody in TNT history has strung wins together like Angus Hume — his 11-match streak is the longest the league has ever seen. For years he was TNT's great nearly-man: runner-up in Season 1 alongside Charlie Simpson, runner-up again in Season 2 with Emerson Wise, forever in the big dance without ever holding the plate. Season 4 finally fixed that. Combining with Luke Sharrock, Hume went through the entire campaign unbeaten — 11-0 — and lifted the championship that had twice slipped through his fingers, adding it to a perfect 3-0 quarter-final record and a 75% career win rate that trails only his partner among the league's regulars. The scouting report is short, because the list of weaknesses is: he gets one more ball back, doesn't blink in the big moments, and quietly wins. Three grand finals in four seasons. The quiet assassin finally has his silverware.
