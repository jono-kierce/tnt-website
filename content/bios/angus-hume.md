Angus Hume wins tennis matches. How, exactly, remains one of the league's enduring mysteries — his personal stat lines are among the most modest of any regular, with just three career aces and a shade over three winners a game — but the scoreboard doesn't lie: a 75% career win rate across 24 matches, behind only Luke Sharrock among established players, including a perfect 8-0 Season 4. Hume's game is the art of subtraction: barely six double faults in three seasons (the lowest rate in the league), few cheap errors, and an uncanny knack for being on the right side of close sets. Season 1 set the tone with a 75% win rate that outperformed flashier names around him. Teammates across Red, Light Blue and Pink have learned the lesson the league keeps relearning: put Angus Hume on your court, and you'll probably win.
