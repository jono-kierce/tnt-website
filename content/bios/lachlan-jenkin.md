Lachlan Jenkin wasted no time making an impression. Arriving in Season 3, he immediately posted one of the rounds' best all-round campaigns — 4.5 winners a game, a 62.5% win rate, and a top-three finish in the season MVP standings, ahead of several long-established stars. The follow-up confirmed it was no fluke: his winner-to-error ratio actually improved in Season 4, and his eleven career aces in just 16 matches mark him as one of the league's most dangerous servers on a per-game basis (albeit one who lives dangerously — 24 double faults say the second serve is a work in progress). Six best-on-court awards from 16 outings is elite recognition for a newcomer. Having already turned out for Orange, Black, Red and Navy, Jenkin heads into Season 5 as perhaps the most complete of the league's newer generation.
