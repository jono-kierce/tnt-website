A genuine Season 1 MVP candidate — the league promoted his clash with Luke Sharrock as a battle of favourites — Jack Raines was the second name called in the inaugural draft and repaid Emerson Wise's faith with a semi-final run for Navy, once landing an absurd 21 first serves in a single match. Season 2 was less kind: partnered with Ted Angel in the infamous "Baz-ball" Green experiment, Raines endured what the TNT Times called a dreadful campaign in Northcote green, though he still walked away with a near-even 7W-8L career record. He hasn't been sighted on a Tuesday since, but the file remains open: quick hands at the net, a big first serve, and unfinished business with the finals.
