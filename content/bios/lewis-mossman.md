The number one pick in the inaugural TNT draft — once dubbed "the Anthony Bennett of TNT," a comparison he has spent four seasons gleefully dismantling. Lewis Mossman is the league's most chaotic server: the undisputed career double-fault king (the "double-fault dossier" is updated weekly in his honour) who somehow also owns three of the top five single-match ace performances in history. The rest of the game is genuine class — top five in career winners, ten Best-on-Ground awards, a 12-winner masterpiece in Season 4 that sits among the greatest single nights ever recorded, and a Season 1 "season-long ban" after a random drug test that we're legally obliged to describe as satire. His best campaign came in Season 3, reaching the grand final with Jimmy Gorton before falling in three sets. Now serving (loosely defined) alongside Emerson Wise in the league's most explosive partnership. Potential, potency, pace — and finally, polish.
