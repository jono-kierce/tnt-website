Lewis Mossman plays tennis with the handbrake off. Second all-time in aces (21) and the only player to twice fire three in a single match, Mossman's serve is among the most feared sights in the league — as is, admittedly, his league-record 50 career double faults. That's the Mossman bargain: everything at full throttle. A four-season veteran with 30 matches across six different teams (nobody has worn more colours), his finest hour came in Season 3 — 75% wins, eight aces, and a top-five MVP finish. He's also durable in the best sense: 120 career winners, nine best-on-court awards, and a knack for lifting when matches get scrappy. Season 4 was leaner, but write him off at your peril. When the first serve is landing, Lewis Mossman turns Tuesday night into a shootout — and he's usually the last one standing.
