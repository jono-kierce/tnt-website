"The Don" owns both ends of the highlight reel: the all-time single-match winners record (a ludicrous 13 against Black in Season 1) and the single-match aces record (four, in Season 3). Not bad for a serve the scouting report said "needs serious open surgery." A captain in every one of TNT's four seasons — no one else can say that — Emerson Wise has led teams in every corner of the ladder, coming agonisingly close to glory as Season 2 runner-up alongside Angus Hume. Tied for the most career games played, he's the league's iron man and one of its great content characters, whether celebrating public holidays mid-rally or hosting romantic corporate-box dinner dates with Lewis Mossman — the partner with whom he now forms what the league bills as the best serving duo TNT has seen. Consistency, banter-vulnerability and thunderous ball-striking: Mr Reliable in every sense but the scoreboard, and working on that.
