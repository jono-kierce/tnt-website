Four games, zero wins, zero MVP votes, one "Least Committed" award, and one immortal record: 22 unforced errors in a single night, a mark that stood as the league's worst until Conor Paraskevas came along. Joel Trudgeon's Season 1 was so complete a catastrophe that the league announced his "brutal delisting" via official channels, with sources confirming he had been told to "never touch a racquet again." Joel reportedly took the news well, telling TNT News he might pick up a new hobby, "such as AFL." His iconic on-court moment was faithfully recreated by Jimmy Gorton two years later — the truest form of legacy. Gone, delisted, never forgotten.
