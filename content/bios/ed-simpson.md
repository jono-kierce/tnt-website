Champion, broadcaster, walking content machine. Ed "J Pod" Simpson won the Season 2 championship alongside Jimmy Gorton, and the pair reunited in Season 4 chasing history as the first duo to win two TNT titles together — falling just short in the semis. In between came genuine hardship: a brutal knee injury from a "footballing tragedy" wiped out his entire third season, a loss felt as keenly in the commentary box as on court (his mic work comes with an official warning: don't listen with headphones). He returned with a point to prove, opening Season 4 with three aces — instantly among the best serving performances ever recorded — and later dropping twelve winners in a single match as a fill-in, arguably the greatest substitute shift in league history. High-volume, high-risk, high-entertainment: the errors column occasionally catches fire, but so does everything else. TNT is simply better with J Pod in it.
