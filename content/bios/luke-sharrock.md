Luke Sharrock doesn't lose. Across four seasons and 26 matches, his win rate sits at a frankly absurd 85% — the best in league history among regulars — capped by a perfect undefeated Season 4. Where others chase highlight reels, Sharrock's genius is pressure: since the league began tracking errors forced, nobody has been better at dragging mistakes out of opponents, season after season. His 17 best-on-court awards trail only Jonathan Kierce despite far fewer games, and his 3-plus votes per match is the highest strike rate in the competition. Season 2 was his masterpiece — 86% wins and 5.4 winners a game — but the truer measure is that he's never had a losing season, never had a bad one, and has turned every team he's joined (Orange, Yellow, Red and Pink so far) into a contender. The league's quiet assassin.
