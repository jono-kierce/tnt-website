If TNT had a Mount Rushmore, Luke "Shiz" Sharrock would be on it twice. A joint inaugural MVP, then back-to-back winner in 2023 with a near-unanimous 41 of a possible 42 votes, he owns an 85% career win rate and more Best-on-Ground awards than anyone in league history. Once dubbed the Local Djokovic — briefly rebranded the Local Kyrgios after a squandered 2024 campaign — his story was three straight years of semi-final heartbreak, until Season 4 rewrote it entirely. Paired with Angus Hume, Sharrock delivered the first perfect season TNT has ever seen: 11-0, minor premiership, championship. He even missed the semi-final with an ankle injury sustained playing beer pong, and Pink won anyway. That's aura. Rumours of an electrician apprenticeship cutting into court time have never slowed him down, and he always — always — knows exactly where the camera is.
