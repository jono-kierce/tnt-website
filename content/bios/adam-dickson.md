The fastest rise in TNT history. Adam "Dicko" Dickson had played just two games — a late Season 1 cameo featuring a 12-winner explosion that still sits near the top of the all-time single-match list — before returning in Season 4 as a first-year captain and promptly winning the MVP. Forty-one votes, edging Jimmy Gorton and Luke Sharrock by one in the closest count the league has seen. Dialling in via video to draft Declan Croucher, he built what was billed as "a dangerous and entertaining duo" and delivered exactly that: Green finished second on the ladder at 6-2, and Dickson's 70% career win rate is bettered by almost nobody. His finals run ended in a quarter-final tiebreak against the eventual grand finalists — about the only thing that didn't go his way all season. On current trajectory, the trophy cabinet is going to need reinforcing.
