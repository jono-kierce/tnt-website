Drafted by Luke Sharrock in Season 1, retired to pursue a media career, and immortalised by the most savage scouting report ever published — the "muffin merchant" whose Season 2 was allegedly derailed by triple choc muffins, scones with butter (and chocolate), and chocolate croissants (with chocolate). Ted Angel's playing record is better than the roast suggests: a perfectly balanced 7W-7L across two seasons, a semi-final run with Orange in Season 1, and some genuinely big hitting nights, including six winners in the season opener. The Season 2 "Baz-ball" experiment with Jack Raines in Northcote green was a bold tactical era that history has judged harshly. Behind the microphone, though, he's undefeated — the voice of the official TNT highlights, and the punchline of jokes he usually wrote himself. Must have been a cracker.
